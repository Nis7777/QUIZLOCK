Lockie

Solve a puzzle to unlock.

Lock the apps you scroll on autopilot. Get them back for a few minutes by solving a chess puzzle, a Sudoku, a quick math question or a 5-letter word.

Support

Need help? Email nismagina@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

  • New iPhone? Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Lockie and tap Settings → Restore Purchases. Your streak and stats come back through iCloud. Then pick the apps to lock again (Apple doesn't move Screen Time selections between devices).
  • Cancel your subscription: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lockie Pro.
  • Apps not locking? Check that Screen Time access is still on: iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → Lockie.
  • No "Tap to solve" notification? Turn notifications on for Lockie in iOS Settings → Notifications → Lockie, and switch on Time Sensitive Notifications so it reaches you during Focus and Sleep. You can always open Lockie yourself: your puzzle is waiting.
  • Back to your app after unlocking: swipe right along the bottom edge of the screen to jump straight back.
  • "Today" card is empty? It fills in as you use your phone; it needs Screen Time access and only works on a real iPhone.
  • Stop locking an app: Settings → App locks → switch it off, or remove it. If strict mode is on, you'll solve three hard puzzles first.
  • Delete your data: in Lockie, Settings → Data → Reset all data.
