Lockie

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Short version: Lockie has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your Screen Time activity never leaves your iPhone. Your progress lives on your phone and in your own iCloud, which we can't access. The only thing that leaves the app is your subscription purchase history, which our subscription provider RevenueCat receives under an anonymous ID.

This policy explains how the Lockie iOS app, its widgets and its extensions ("Lockie", "we", "us") handle information. Lockie is published on the App Store by Tomás Beja, Portugal, who is responsible for this app and its data ("we", "us"). Contact: nismagina@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Almost nothing leaves your device. Lockie doesn't run any server, has no sign-up or login, and contains no advertising or tracking code. The only exception is your purchase history (section 6), which our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives under an anonymous ID. We don't use any data to track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell it.

2. Screen Time and the apps you lock

Lockie uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) to block the apps you choose. Apple gives Lockie only opaque tokens for the apps, categories and websites you select, so we can't see which apps they are.

The "Today" screen-time card is drawn by Apple's DeviceActivityReport system inside a protected extension. Lockie's main app can't read those numbers, and they never leave your device. Your selections, unlock timers and lock schedules are stored only on your iPhone.

3. Information you give in the app

During setup you can optionally enter:

  • a first name or nickname
  • answers to a few questions (for example how much you scroll, how scrolling makes you feel, your goals, where you heard about Lockie, and which puzzles you like)

These are used only inside the app to personalise your plan and messages. You can skip the name.

4. Progress and settings

The following are stored on your device:

  • your puzzle progress, streaks and stats (puzzles solved, unlocks, time saved)
  • your daily puzzle history and difficulty
  • your settings

5. iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, Lockie syncs your progress, settings and setup answers (including your name, if you entered one) through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they're restored on a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and we have no access to it.

Screen Time selections are never synced. To turn sync off, disable iCloud for Lockie in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Lockie Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your Apple ID has an active subscription.

To understand how subscriptions are doing (for example how many free trials become subscriptions), Lockie uses RevenueCat. When you start a trial, subscribe, renew or restore, RevenueCat receives the purchase details from Apple (product, price, dates and country) linked to a random anonymous ID that Lockie creates. It doesn't receive your name, email, Apple ID or your Screen Time data, and it isn't used for advertising or tracking.

7. Notifications

Lockie only sends local notifications created on your device: the "Tap to solve" notification when you tap "Solve to unlock" on a locked app (marked Time Sensitive if you allow it, so it can reach you during Focus), streak reminders, the weekly summary and the free-trial reminder. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings. No push server is used.

8. Feedback and support emails

If you contact us or send feedback, it goes through your own email app. We receive your email address, the message and the basic device information shown in the draft (app and iOS version). We use them only to reply and to improve Lockie, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

9. App Store ratings

Lockie may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple.

10. Children

Lockie is a self-management tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone; the only data that leaves the app is the anonymous purchase history described in section 6.

11. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Data → Reset all data in the app to erase your locks, settings, setup answers, stats and streaks from this device and from your iCloud copy. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Reset doesn\'t reach RevenueCat\'s purchase records, because they\'re tied to an anonymous ID only Apple and RevenueCat can match. To have them deleted, email us from the app (Settings → Contact support) and we\'ll ask RevenueCat to delete them.

Cancelling a subscription is done in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions; resetting data doesn't cancel it.

12. Your rights (GDPR)

Because Lockie keeps your data on your device and in your own iCloud, you control it directly. The data we do hold is support emails and, through RevenueCat, anonymous purchase records. We process the purchase records to provide and measure the subscription (our legitimate interest and the performance of your subscription), and support emails to answer you. You can ask us to access, correct or delete them at nismagina@gmail.com.

RevenueCat, Inc. is based in the United States and processes purchase records for us as a data processor, under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD, cnpd.pt).

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date. If a change means we'd start collecting data, we'll ask for your consent in the app first.
