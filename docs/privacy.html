QuizLock

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Short version: QuizLock has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your Screen Time activity never leaves your iPhone. The only copy of your progress outside your phone is in your own iCloud, and we can't access it.

This policy explains how the QuizLock iOS app, its widgets and its extensions ("QuizLock", "we", "us") handle information. QuizLock is developed by Dinis Magina, Portugal. Contact: nismagina@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. QuizLock doesn't run any server, has no sign-up or login, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We don't use your data to track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell or share it.

2. Screen Time and the apps you lock

QuizLock uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) to block the apps you choose. Apple gives QuizLock only opaque tokens for the apps, categories and websites you select, so we can't see which apps they are.

The "Today" screen-time card is drawn by Apple's DeviceActivityReport system inside a protected extension. QuizLock's main app can't read those numbers, and they never leave your device. Your selections, unlock timers and lock schedules are stored only on your iPhone.

3. Information you give in the app

During setup you can optionally enter:

  • a first name or nickname
  • answers to a few questions (for example how much you scroll, how scrolling makes you feel, your goals, where you heard about QuizLock, and which puzzles you like)

These are used only inside the app to personalise your plan and messages. You can skip the name.

4. Progress and settings

The following are stored on your device:

  • your puzzle progress, streaks and stats (puzzles solved, unlocks, time saved)
  • your daily puzzle history and difficulty
  • your settings

5. iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, QuizLock syncs your progress, settings and setup answers (including your name, if you entered one) through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they're restored on a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and we have no access to it.

Screen Time selections are never synced. To turn sync off, disable iCloud for QuizLock in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

QuizLock Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your Apple ID has an active subscription. Apple may share anonymised, aggregated sales reports with developers.

7. Notifications

QuizLock only sends local notifications created on your device: the puzzle nudge when you tap "Start Puzzle", streak reminders, the weekly summary and the free-trial reminder. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings. No push server is used.

8. Feedback and support emails

If you contact us or send feedback, it goes through your own email app. We receive your email address, the message and the basic device information shown in the draft (app and iOS version). We use them only to reply and to improve QuizLock, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

9. App Store ratings

QuizLock may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple.

10. Children

QuizLock is a self-management tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, and the app doesn't send any data to us.

11. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Data → Reset all data in the app to erase your locks, settings, setup answers, stats and streaks from this device and from your iCloud copy. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Cancelling a subscription is done in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions; resetting data doesn't cancel it.

12. Your rights (GDPR)

Because QuizLock keeps your data on your device and in your own iCloud, you control it directly. For anything we do hold, such as support emails, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it at nismagina@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD, cnpd.pt).

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date. If a change means we'd start collecting data, we'll ask for your consent in the app first.
