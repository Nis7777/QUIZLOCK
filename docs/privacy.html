Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Short version: QuizLock works on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't sell data, and your Screen Time activity never leaves your iPhone.

This policy explains how the QuizLock iOS app ("QuizLock", "we", "us") handles information. QuizLock is developed by Dinis Magina, based in Portugal. Contact: nismagina@gmail.com.

1. Screen Time and the apps you lock

QuizLock uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) to block the apps you choose. Apple gives QuizLock only opaque tokens for the apps and categories you select. We can't see which apps they are, how long you use them, or any of their content. These tokens, your unlock settings and timers are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

2. Puzzles and progress

Your puzzle answers, progress, settings and free-unlock count are stored locally on your device. We don't collect them.

2a. iCloud sync (no accounts)

QuizLock has no accounts. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple ID, QuizLock keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it survives a new phone: your streaks, stats and daily puzzle history, which puzzles and difficulty you use, your unlock time settings, your theme, whether you've finished setup, and your onboarding answers (such as the name you entered and your goals). This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it and it's never sent to us. Your Screen Time selections are never synced. If iCloud is off, everything stays on your device.

To delete everything, use Settings → Data → Reset all data in the app. It removes your locks, settings, stats and streaks from your device and from iCloud. Your subscription isn't affected.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active QuizLock Pro subscription.

4. No analytics, ads or tracking

QuizLock contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We don't collect usage data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. If this ever changes, we'll ask for your permission first and update this policy before it happens.

5. Feedback emails

If you send feedback, it goes through your own email app. We receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and improve the app.

6. Notifications

QuizLock sends local notifications, created on your device, to take you to your puzzle. No push-notification server or account is involved.

7. Children

QuizLock isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

Because almost everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud, you control it: Settings → Data → Reset all data deletes it from both, and deleting the app removes the copy on your device. For anything we do hold, such as feedback emails, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it by emailing nismagina@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date.
