# Reference screenshots (inspiration only)

These are 40 screens of the app that inspired ours. Use them to understand the flow, the screen structure and the level of polish. **Don't copy them.** Our app has its own identity:

- **Brand colour:** coral #FF5A36 (not their red).
- **Logo:** a puzzle-piece padlock (`ArtSource/AppIcon.png`), not their key.
- **Mascot:** our coral padlock character (`ArtSource/mascot_pointing.png`), not their boy in a red t-shirt.
- **Name and wordmark:** come from the CONFIG block in PROMPT.md.
- **Challenges:** our 4 puzzle games (Chess, Sudoku, Math, Word), not the SAT/Art/Guitar quizzes shown here.
- **Copy:** says "puzzle" instead of "quiz".

Also ignore the black "appllama" pill at the top left of each screenshot. It's a screen-recording overlay, not part of the app. Screens 17, 18 and 20 are Apple's own system sheets; iOS shows those, so don't rebuild them.
