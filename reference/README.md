# Reference screenshots (visual target)

These are the 40 screens of the original app. Use them to match the layout, spacing, colours, typography and animations as closely as possible.

What's different in our app (PROMPT.md always wins):
- The challenges are our 4 puzzle games (Chess, Sudoku, Math, Word), not the SAT/Art/Guitar quizzes shown here.
- The copy says "puzzle" instead of "quiz", and the app name/brand comes from the CONFIG block.
- Ignore the black "appllama" pill at the top left of each screenshot. It's a screen-recording overlay, not part of the app.
- Screens 17, 18 and 20 are Apple's own system sheets (Screen Time permission and the FamilyActivityPicker). Don't rebuild them; iOS shows them.
