# Screenshot captions

Generated by the `Screenshots` scheme (UI test `ScreenshotTests`) on the iPhone 16 Pro Max Simulator:
6.9" display, 1320 × 2868 px, saved to `AppStore/screenshots/en/`.
Add the captions on top in a design tool, or use them as the screenshot text in App Store Connect.

How to regenerate:
1. In Xcode, choose the **Screenshots** scheme and the **iPhone 16 Pro Max** Simulator.
2. Press ⌘U. Each run relaunches the app with `-demoData -screen <name>` (sample stats, a 12-day streak and
   Simulator sample apps) and saves the PNGs.
